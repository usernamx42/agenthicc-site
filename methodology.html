The Agenthicc Index / v1

A score is an argument.
Here is ours.

The Index is our editorial opinion about a specific product or feature, at a stated version and date. It is not a universal ranking of AI.

AxisWeightWhat we ask
Usefulness30%Does it solve the stated problem in ordinary use?
Quality25%How usable and consistent is the result?
Originality15%Is the added capability meaningful?
Trust and transparency20%Can we understand the limits, provenance and controls?
Value10%Does the benefit justify the verified cost and effort?

Two evidence labels. Always visible.

TESTED We operated the exact version and publish a dated test log, conditions, attempts and limitations. Only these items can become recommendations.

DESK-SCORED We reviewed primary documentation and other evidence. We did not run the product. This is research and opinion, not a test or recommendation.

How the total works

Each axis receives 0–5 in half-point steps. Zero means the assessed task fails; three means useful with clear tradeoffs; five means exceptional under the defined conditions. We multiply each axis divided by five by its percentage weight, then add and round to one decimal. If an axis lacks evidence, we do not publish a total.

Beats have separate leaderboards. We do not derive scores from other channels’ YouTube statistics. Material updates can trigger a new assessment; the earlier assessment remains visible with the reason for change.

Money and independence

We may earn advertising, sponsorship or clearly disclosed affiliate revenue. Commercial relationships never enter the scoring formula. The video and assessment identify any relevant relationship. There are no paid rankings.

Corrections

When a published claim is contradicted, we review the evidence and prepare a dated correction for the Editor. Approved corrections appear with the original video or assessment. The change log will identify what changed and why. No assessments or corrections have been published yet.
